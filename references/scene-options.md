# Scene-aware option builder

Use this reference only after inspecting the photograph. The aim is to give the user meaningful creative control without making them art-direct from a blank page.

## Build a short recommendation

State what the image offers in one sentence, then recommend one combination:

> This low-angle city photo has open sky and a strong roofline, so an oversized character peeking from behind the building will use the perspective naturally.

Follow with choices for the unresolved decisions. Keep the complete menu compact enough to answer in one message.

## Map scene evidence to choices

| Photograph evidence | Suitable roles/actions | Suitable clothing cues | Avoid by default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rain, wet road, umbrellas | puddle jumper, commuter, sheep/cloud vignette | raincoat, boots, hood, bright waterproof accent | sandals, dry-weather lounging |
| Snow, mountains, visible cold | explorer, skier, warm drink carrier | puffer, knit cap, gloves, snow boots | sleeveless summer outfit |
| Strong sun, park, summer street | cyclist, skater, walker, photographer | loose shirt, shorts or wide trousers, cap, sneakers | heavy winter layers |
| Transit interior | seated dreamer, reader, commuter, small companion | practical layers, tote, headphones | city-giant scale that blocks the carriage |
| Water, lake, coast | fisher, bird rider, swimmer, quiet observer | windbreaker, relaxed layers, hat | unsupported standing on water unless fantastical |
| Tall buildings, low-angle view | peeking giant, rooftop sitter, long-legged walker | bold oversized silhouette | small central figure lost against architecture |
| Landmark or tower | explorer, playful climber, world-expansion ensemble | landmark-contrasting palette | covering the identifying silhouette entirely |
| Aerial city view | scattered residents, giant picnic, neighborhood ensemble | varied but coordinated palette | one tiny detailed figure |
| Empty wall or façade | leaning figure, emerging character, mural-like color spill | simple large blocks | excessive facial micro-detail |
| Poster-ready negative space | editorial giant, campaign character | fashion-forward high contrast | uncontrolled text or unreadable typography |

## Character presentation choices

Offer only choices that materially change the result. A useful pattern is:

- **A — Boy/man-presenting:** describe age range, silhouette, hair, and scene-specific outfit.
- **B — Girl/woman-presenting:** describe age range, silhouette, hair, and scene-specific outfit.
- **C — Androgynous/non-specified:** describe a distinct silhouette and styling, not merely a neutral placeholder.
- **D — Animal/fantasy alternative:** include only if it suits the scene or the user asked for imaginative options.

Do not infer the user's gender or ask them to justify a presentation choice. When a real person is in the photograph, ask whether the illustration should represent that person, replace them, accompany them, or remain an unrelated fictional character.

## Outfit generation

Each outfit option should specify:

- garment silhouette;
- two or three main colors plus an accent;
- footwear appropriate to the action;
- at most one or two props;
- texture treatment: flat, pencil, crayon, checks, stripes, or minimal hatching.

Create contrast with the background while remaining plausible. Against a gray city, offer warm bright accents; against a colorful façade, simplify the palette; against open blue sky, avoid losing the silhouette in similar blues.

## Placement generation

Offer placements that name a visible anchor:

- sit on the front railing;
- step between the two buildings;
- peek over the right roofline;
- ride along the road's perspective line;
- float above the water while partially overlapping the pier;
- occupy the upper-left sky without covering the landmark.

Avoid vague placements such as “in the middle” or “somewhere in the background.”

## Example interaction template

Adapt the wording; do not copy it mechanically:

> I recommend **B2 + P1**: a woman-presenting city explorer in a coral windbreaker and cream wide-leg trousers, leaning over the right-side railing. The warm palette separates her from the cool overcast background, and the railing supplies a believable contact point.
>
> **Character:** A. playful young man / B. city explorer woman / C. androgynous cyclist
>
> **Scale & placement:** P1. oversized at the railing / P2. human-scale walking on the path / P3. small companion near the bench
>
> **Finish:** F1. clean flat color / F2. pencil grain / F3. lively crayon doodles

If the user says “you decide,” choose the recommended combination and proceed. If image generation is not yet requested, return a concise art direction rather than changing the image.
